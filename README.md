A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. Three hundred years keeping the Ember Crown lit, and all it gives back is a cold draught and a creaking throne. The tiredness lives in the pauses and deflections — the stairs, the sleeping guard, the ravens on rest, the curse that forgot its own words — never in a plain statement of weariness. It ends with a sting: strike, or fetch him a chair and talk terms.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output/entry.txt`).
